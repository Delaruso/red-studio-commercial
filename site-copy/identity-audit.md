# Identity Audit Offer

**Price:** Free
**Timeline:** 48 hours
**Deliverable:** One-page PDF + 3-minute video

---

## What the client sends

1. Current logo or brand assets
2. One product or service they’re most proud of
3. One brand they admire and why

## What you deliver

**One-page PDF:**
- What their current identity says vs. what it should say
- One visual direction that would change their market position
- Three words their brand should own

**3-minute video:**
- Screen recording with voiceover
- Show, don’t tell: point to their current assets, then sketch the direction
- End with one clear next step: the Sovereign Identity Package or a smaller starter

## Why this works

- Zero risk for the client
- Proves you can see what they can’t
- Creates the gap: they see their current brand, then they see what’s possible
- Converts 30–50% into paid projects if the audit is sharp

## How to deliver fast

**Template structure:**
1. **What you have** — describe their current brand in one sentence
2. **What it says** — the unconscious message their brand sends now
3. **What it could say** — the myth or feeling it should evoke
4. **One direction** — show a color, a mark, a word
5. **Next step** — clear offer, no pressure

**Time breakdown:**
- 30 min review
- 20 min PDF
- 30 min video edit
- Total: 1.5 hours per audit

**Batch audits:** Do 2–3 per week. They take less time as you build templates.

## Upsell path

After audit:
- Starter: identity direction document only — €500
- Full: Sovereign Identity Package — €2,500
- Monthly: myth maintenance — €300/month

## Outreach template

Subject: Your brand has a blind spot

Body:
```
Hey [name],

I’ve been following [brand/project] for a while. The product is solid, but the identity doesn’t match what I see when I use it.

I do identity audits for brands like yours—free, no strings. I’ll send you a one-page breakdown of what’s possible and a 3-minute video showing the direction.

If it’s useful, great. If not, no hard feelings.

Either way, you get clarity on your brand.

Want me to run it?
```

Send 20 per week. 2–3 replies = 1–2 conversions.
